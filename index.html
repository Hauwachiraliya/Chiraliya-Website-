About Chiraliya

Chiraliya Children Care & Inclusion Foundation is a community-focused organization committed to creating opportunities for vulnerable people and building stronger, more inclusive communities.

What We Do

Our Mission

To improve the lives of vulnerable children, people with disabilities, women and communities by providing support, education, inclusion and empowerment while promoting dignity and equal opportunities.

Our Vision

A society where every child, woman and person with a disability can live with dignity, access opportunities and participate fully in their community.
