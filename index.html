About Us

Chiraliya Children Care and Inclusion Foundation is a community-focused organization committed to supporting vulnerable children, orphans, people with disabilities and women. We believe that every person deserves dignity, opportunity and a chance to build a better future.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide meaningful support, educational opportunities, social inclusion and empowerment to vulnerable people while working with communities to create lasting positive change.

What We Do

Children & Orphans

Supporting vulnerable children and orphans with education, care, basic needs and opportunities for a brighter future.

Disability Inclusion

Promoting dignity, inclusion, education and equal opportunities for people living with disabilities.

Women Empowerment

Supporting women and vulnerable families through empowerment, skills development and community initiatives.
