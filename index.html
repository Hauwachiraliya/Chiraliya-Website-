Every Child Deserves a Chance.

CHIRALIYA Children Care & Inclusion Foundation is a Nigerian community-focused organisation working to support orphans, vulnerable children, women and persons with disabilities through education, social inclusion, wellbeing, empowerment and humanitarian assistance.

Our focus

We believe that every child should have the opportunity to learn, participate, feel safe and build a hopeful future.

Location: Maiduguri, Borno State, Nigeria

Priority: Vulnerable children and children with disabilities

About CHIRALIYA

Building opportunity, dignity and inclusion.

CHIRALIYA was established to help children and other vulnerable people overcome barriers to education, participation and wellbeing. We work with communities and partners to turn practical support into lasting opportunities.

Our Vision

A society where every child, regardless of disability, vulnerability or background, has the opportunity to learn, grow, participate and thrive.

Our Mission

To improve the educational, social and overall wellbeing of vulnerable children and persons with disabilities through inclusive programmes, empowerment, advocacy and community support.

Our Approach

We listen to communities, identify practical needs, work with partners and aim to deliver transparent, accountable and measurable support.
